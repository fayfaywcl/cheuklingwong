About me

Designing at the meeting point of code and creativity.

I’m a Joint Major student in Computer Science and Computation Arts at Concordia University. I’m interested in how technology can support expressive, clear, and engaging experiences.

My work spans game development, UI/UX, AI-driven projects, and visual communication. I enjoy moving between the big idea and the details that make it work: shaping an interaction, building it in Unity or on the web, then refining it through feedback.

Across collaborative projects, freelance design, and creative technology, I bring curiosity, care for the user experience, and a willingness to experiment.
