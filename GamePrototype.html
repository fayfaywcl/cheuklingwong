Project Overview

A first-person Unity collectathon that brings together walking, jumping, a child camera, and pickups in a simple world built from square shapes.

Watch Gameplay Demo

From practice to a playable idea

After finishing tutorials and individual experiments, I wanted to check whether I had actually understood what I learned. Instead of following another guide, I built a compact original prototype using the systems I had practiced during the week.

First-person movementChild cameraJumpingRotating collectiblesPickup effectsBlock environment

Putting the systems together

I built a simple environment from square shapes, then brought movement, a child camera, jumping, and collectible objects into the same scene. The collectibles rotate and respond when picked up, so the player can see and feel the interaction rather than only test each component in isolation.

My main check was whether the separate systems could work together as a small playable loop. They did: I could move through the environment, jump, and collect objects. The visuals are basic, but this prototype met my goal for the week and confirmed that I understood how the components connect.

This was my first independent check after the tutorials: a small prototype built from the mechanics I had practiced, with all of them working together.

What this prototype helped me learn

Keeping the scope small made it possible to finish and evaluate the result. I could focus on how the camera, movement, environment, and pickups interacted without taking on a larger game idea before I was ready.
